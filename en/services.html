What we do

Three services, one supplier.

01

Aircraft parts sales

We supply parts for aircraft in general and consumables. Every part is identified by name, part number, serial number and category, and is FAA-certified.

  • Airframe & structures
  • Engines
  • Avionics: navigation, communication and instruments
  • Oils, lubricants and special products
  • Signage
02

Aircraft parts maintenance

Our qualified, well-trained technical staff, with many years of experience, maintains aircraft parts in general.

  • Qualified technical staff
  • Aircraft parts in general
  • Service in six cities across Ecuador
03

Ground support equipment (GSE)

Sales and maintenance of parts for the ground support equipment that keeps your operation running.

  • Ground starters
  • Tires
  • Stairs
  • Other support equipment
