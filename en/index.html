FAQ

What our clients ask.

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

What kind of aircraft parts do you sell?

Airframe, engine and avionics parts (navigation, communication, flight and engine instruments), signage and consumables such as oils, lubricants and special products. We also supply parts for ground support equipment: starters, tires, stairs and more.

Do your prices include VAT?

Yes. All our prices include VAT. We sell by quotation because prices vary depending on the type of item.

Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes, we offer volume and wholesale discounts. Include the quantities when you request your quote.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit and credit notes.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship domestically and internationally. We are present in Quito, Guayaquil, Manta, Portoviejo, El Coca and Shell-Mera, and we operate with the United States, Russia and Thailand.

What does AOG mean and how long does delivery take?

AOG (Aircraft on Ground) means an aircraft cannot fly until it receives a part. Delivery time depends on the client’s needs: AOG orders are handled with priority, and we also serve orders with standard lead times.

Are your parts certified and under warranty?

Our products are FAA-certified. The warranty depends on each product and is specified in the quote.
