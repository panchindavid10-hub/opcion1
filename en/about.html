About us

Serving aviation in Ecuador since 2018.

We are an Ecuadorian company dedicated to the sale and maintenance of parts for aircraft and ground support equipment.

Who we are

Parts and maintenance with technical backing.

Muso Business was founded in 2018. From the start, we have focused on the maintenance and sale of parts for aircraft ground support equipment, and on the maintenance and sale of aircraft parts in general.

What sets us apart is our people: qualified, well-trained technical staff with many years of experience across all our areas. That experience lets us understand what your operation needs and respond on time, even when an aircraft is on the ground (AOG).

Today we are present in six cities across Ecuador and operate with the United States, Russia and Thailand.
